## About Me

<img src="/images/头像.jpg" class="floatpic" style="width:160px; height:auto;">

<p style="text-indent:2em;">
I am Qiang Wang, born in February 2002. I am currently a master's student in Transportation Engineering at Wuhan University of Technology, enrolled in September 2024. My research focuses on planning and control for autonomous driving.
</p>

<p style="text-indent:2em;">
During my graduate study, I work on autonomous driving planning and control, including high-definition map route search, behavior decision-making, local trajectory generation, trajectory tracking control, and safety constraint handling in complex traffic scenarios. I am familiar with ROS, C++, OpenDRIVE, CasADi/IPOPT, and related development frameworks, with experience in autonomous driving algorithm development, simulation validation, and <strong>real-vehicle debugging</strong>.
</p>
